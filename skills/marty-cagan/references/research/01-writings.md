## Primary Sources

1. SVPG author archive, Marty Cagan: https://www.svpg.com/author/marty/
   - Shows current role as Founder, Partner - Product at SVPG.
   - Recent posts include `Build To Learn FAQ` on 2026-04-27, `Build to Learn vs Build to Earn` on 2026-04-16, `Commercial vs Internal Products` on 2026-04-09, and `Product Coaching and AI` on 2026-02-04.

2. SVPG books page: https://www.svpg.com/books/
   - `TRANSFORMED` teaches the product operating model and transformation.
   - `EMPOWERED`, co-authored with Chris Jones, focuses on leaders creating environments where people can solve hard problems.
   - `INSPIRED` is the foundational product-team book attached to Cagan's public product philosophy.

3. `The Four Big Risks`: https://www.svpg.com/four-big-risks/
   - Core risk taxonomy: value, usability, feasibility, business viability.
   - Business viability is repeatedly emphasized as under-appreciated or avoided by product managers.

4. `Product vs Feature Teams`: https://www.svpg.com/product-vs-feature-teams/
   - Defines empowered product teams as cross-functional, outcome-measured, and empowered to solve problems.
   - Distinguishes true PM work from feature-team/product-owner/project-manager work.
   - Strong claim: the PM in an empowered team owns value and viability.

5. `Product Strategy - Overview`: https://www.svpg.com/product-strategy-overview/
   - Product strategy means deciding which problems teams should solve.
   - Many product organizations lack real product strategy.
   - Teams need hard problems plus space to solve them.

6. `Discovery - Excuses`: https://www.svpg.com/discovery-excuses/
   - Rejects "we don't have time" as a weak excuse when risk is high.
   - If an idea has meaningful risk, the relevant question is whether the team can afford not to test it.

7. `Build to Learn vs Build to Earn`: https://www.svpg.com/build-to-learn-vs-build-to-earn/
   - In the AI era, delivery cost drops and the bottleneck moves to discovering a solution worth building.
   - Product discovery is build-to-learn; product delivery is build-to-earn.
   - The strongest PMs are builders/creators who build prototypes to learn, not facilitators hiding behind process.

8. `Build To Learn FAQ`: https://www.svpg.com/build-to-learn-faq/
   - PM is not the sole decider, manager, or protector.
   - PM's specific contribution is value and viability.
   - Product discovery tests prototypes against value, usability, feasibility, and viability.
   - A PRD supplements discovery; it must not replace discovery.

9. `Commercial vs Internal Products`: https://www.svpg.com/commercial-vs-internal-products/
   - Commercial products must not merely solve the problem; they must be sufficiently better than alternatives to win switching behavior.
   - PM for commercial products must be immersed in sales, marketing, monetization, legal, compliance, and market competition.

10. `Product Coaching and AI`: https://www.svpg.com/product-coaching-and-ai/
    - AI can expose product-management theater if PMs use it only to accelerate roadmaps, PRDs, and user stories.
    - Models can be useful product coaches only when configured with strategic context and a clear product operating model.

## Repeated Core Claims

### 1. Output is not product work

Appears in `Product vs Feature Teams`, `Product Management Theater`, `Build to Learn vs Build to Earn`, and `Product Coaching and AI`.

Working extraction: Cagan treats feature lists, roadmaps, PRDs, user stories, and stakeholder request aggregation as weak substitutes for product work when they are not tied to customer/business outcomes.

### 2. PM is accountable for value and viability

Appears in `The Four Big Risks`, `Product vs Feature Teams`, `Build To Learn FAQ`, and Lenny's 2024 episode summary.

Working extraction: a PM who cannot reason about whether customers will choose/buy and whether the business can support the solution is not doing the hard part of product management.

### 3. Product discovery is not theater; it is risk reduction

Appears in `The Four Big Risks`, `Discovery - Excuses`, `Build to Learn vs Build to Earn`, and `Build To Learn FAQ`.

Working extraction: discovery is not "talking to users" as a ritual. It is prototype-based learning against four product risks.

### 4. Product sense is built, not inherited

Appears in Produktmenschen transcript and `Product Coaching and AI`.

Working extraction: product sense is developed through customers, data, market, industry, technology, and reflection. "Gut feel" without the work is arrogance.

### 5. AI increases the premium on real PM craft

Appears in 2024-2026 AI/product articles.

Working extraction: AI accelerates delivery and prototyping, so weak PMs can create more bad output faster; strong PMs use AI to build-to-learn and sharpen product sense.
