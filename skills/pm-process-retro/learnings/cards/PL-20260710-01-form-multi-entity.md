# PL-20260710-01 — Form / multi-entity depth vs macro flow

## Combat story

During design of the WEN PM skill flow itself, a realistic product case surfaced: suppliers must submit assets under a WhatsApp Business Manager with **multiple WABAs**, heavy per-WABA fields, **conditional expansion** (phone selected → phone detail attributes; partner selected → partner linkage), possible **reference-pane IA** (stable FB context left, long WABA form right), multi-WABA copy, and a desire to “lock PRD + flowchart + wireframe consensus before continuing.”

The concern was correct in direction: pure outcome-level grilling can stop at “provider submits WABA assets” and never force field linkage, multi-entity operations, or usability prototypes.

## Process failure

- No durable place to record “we under-grilled form altitude.”
- No ACTIVE force list that core skills must load.
- Risk of the opposite theater: treating full visual/wireframe agreement as product evidence.

## Forced next time

See `learnings/ACTIVE.md` scenario **multi-entity or multi-section asset forms**.

## Non-goals

- Not a requirement to ship left-fixed / right-scroll layout.
- Not a requirement to use module background colors.
- Not permission to Build from consensus alone.
